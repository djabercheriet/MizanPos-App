## Your phone. Your scanner. Your POS.

MIZANE Mobile Scanner is a companion Android application that connects your smartphone to **MIZANE POS** and turns its camera into a wireless barcode scanner.

Scan a product using your phone and send its barcode directly to your Windows POS. The product is processed by the connected POS, making checkout faster and more convenient without requiring a dedicated handheld barcode scanner.

## Features

- **Wireless Barcode Scanning** — Scan product barcodes using your Android camera.
- **Direct POS Integration** — Send scanned codes to the connected MIZANE POS.
- **Fast Cart Updates** — Repeated scans of a product can increase its cart quantity.
- **Scan History** — Review previous scans directly on your phone.
- **Device Pairing** — Pair and manage your mobile connection with the POS.
